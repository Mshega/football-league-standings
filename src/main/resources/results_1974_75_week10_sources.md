## Remaining uncertainty

- Newcastle v Ipswich (1–0) and Chelsea v Wolves (0–1) were originally read
  from the Italian Wikipedia results matrix and have since been corrected
  externally. Neither corrected score has yet been confirmed against an
  independent primary source.
- The Rothmans Book of Football League Records is the primary authority cited
  by all Wikipedia articles; direct access to that volume would constitute
  full verification of all 11 results.

## Scoring rules

The data is processed under the historical rules:
- **Win = 2 points, Draw = 1 point, Loss = 0 points** (2-point win system,
  in use until 1981/82)
- **Goal average** (GF ÷ GA) as the tiebreaker, not goal difference
  (goal average was used from 1894/95 until 1975/76)
